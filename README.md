# 19 November 2026 — The World in One Page

A newspaper-style front page that sums up Thursday 19 November 2026 in a single 1024×1024 image: six stories, six panels, one day — from Vice City's return to a spacecraft one light-day from home. Built for make.fun's "Sum up 19 November's world news in one image" bounty ($250 in USDC).

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only, no network) deterministically copies the bundled `entry.png` to the directory in `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`).

## The day's stories in the image

1. **Grand Theft Auto VI launches worldwide** — Vice City returns after a 13-year wait; the global rollout begins at midnight, starting in New Zealand. (Sources: Reuters; People)
2. **Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral summit in Shenzhen** — the first three-way meeting of the three leaders, held on the sidelines of the APEC summit. (Sources: Chinese Foreign Ministry; Reuters)
3. **Fed minutes point to more rate hikes** — minutes of the 27–28 October meeting, released 18 November, show almost all officials expect further hikes if inflation and the labour market develop as expected; rates were held at 3.75–4%. (Source: Federal Reserve)
4. **Nvidia's new chief** — founder Jensen Huang has resigned; interim CEO/CFO Colette Kress promises December pay rises and more stock grants, and Nvidia has stopped shipping licensed Hopper (H200) chips to China. (Sources: NVIDIA; Reuters)
5. **Hakeem Jeffries set to make history** — House Democrats nominate him 251–11 for Speaker; if elected by the full House on 3 January, he becomes the first Black Speaker. (Sources: Associated Press; NPR)
6. **Voyager 1, one light-day away** — the probe reaches about 16 billion miles from Earth, the first human-made object one light-day away; NASA confirms the milestone on 19 November. (Source: NASA)
